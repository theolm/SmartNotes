# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

android

Material 3 everywhere, shipping on Android, iOS, and macOS. Respect each OS's
permissions, safe-area insets, accessibility, and navigation guarantees.

## Users

Personal journalers — individuals capturing their own thoughts, ideas, and diary-style reflections by speaking instead of typing. They reach for the app in the moment a thought occurs: walking, commuting, winding down. The context is often one-handed, distracted, or low-light, so the core action must work with almost no attention. The journal is private by definition; the user is the only audience.

## Product Purpose

SmartNotes saves spoken thoughts as editable transcriptions with near-zero effort, so the user builds a lasting voice-journaling habit. Optional AI organization is available inside a note, with a preview before replacing the user's words. Success is measured by return: the user keeps coming back day after day because capturing an entry costs seconds and the timeline quietly accumulates into a personal record worth keeping. Habit, not volume or organization, is the metric that matters.

## Positioning

Zero-friction voice-to-note: tap, speak, tap to stop. No typing, no filing, no organizing required. Every screen reinforces that capture is effortless. Initial setup downloads speech and embedding models; a formatting model is optional, selected from a small on-device catalog, and installed only with explicit consent. Transcription completes the note; indexing runs in the background. AI organization is a separate, preview-first action that requires a selected formatting model. Recordings, notes, and queries stay on the device, and existing journals remain readable while missing models are installed or repaired.

## Brand Personality

Calm, private, unobtrusive. The app should feel like a paper notebook that happens to listen — quiet chrome, no noise, nothing competing with the user's own words. Voice and tone are gentle and plain; the interface recedes so the journal content leads.

## Anti-references

- Not a productivity dashboard: no charts, metrics, streaks-as-pressure, or work-app chrome.
- Not a social app: no feeds, likes, sharing loops, or gamification noise.
- Not a bloated note app: no endless toolbars, folder trees, or formatting clutter competing with capture.

## Design Principles

- Capture is the product. The record action dominates; everything else is secondary chrome that never slows the path from thought to note.
- Save first, enrich later. The original voice is authoritative; AI changes require acceptance and never replace the retained recording or original transcription.
- Recede, don't decorate. Minimal surfaces, system typography, no visual noise — the user's words are the content and the design.
- Private by feel, not just by policy. The interface should read as personal and sealed: no social patterns, no exposure anxiety.
- Effortless return. Timeline, search, and detail exist to make past entries easy to revisit, reinforcing the habit loop.
- Functional inversion only. Dark mode mirrors the light system exactly; no second personality after sunset.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Baseline Material 3 defaults: standard contrast ratios, minimum touch targets, and platform accessibility services. No special requirements beyond what the Material 3 theme provides.
